2. Conversion accuracy and formatting

PDF-to-Word conversion is not always exact. The current tool primarily extracts selectable text and places it into a DOCX document. It may not preserve the original page layout, tables, images, columns, fonts, spacing, headers, footers or page breaks.

3. Scanned and protected PDFs

Scanned documents that contain images of text may require Optical Character Recognition (OCR). The current converter does not provide OCR. Password-protected, damaged, unusually structured or very large PDFs may fail to convert.

4. Check the output

Always review the converted document before relying on it, sharing it, printing it or submitting it. For official, legal, academic, financial or business documents, compare the output with the original PDF and correct any errors.
