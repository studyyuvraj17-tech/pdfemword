Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information may be handled when you visit PDFemWord and use its tools.

1. Overview

PDFemWord aims to minimise the information needed to use its browser-based PDF converter. This policy describes the current website and must be updated if new tools, analytics, advertising or other services are added.

2. PDF files and local processing

The current PDF-to-Word converter reads the PDF you select in your browser and creates a DOCX file on your device. The converter does not upload the selected PDF or its extracted text to a PDFemWord server. Your file remains under your control in your browser session.

This statement applies to the current converter only. Other tools or third-party services may handle data differently. Do not use an online tool with documents you are not authorised to process.

3. Technical information

When you visit a website, your browser and hosting provider may process standard technical information such as an IP address, browser type, device information, requested page and request time for delivery, security and troubleshooting. PDFemWord does not currently require an account to use the converter.

4. Cookies, analytics and advertising

The website may use technologies needed to deliver pages. If analytics or advertising services (including Google AdSense) are enabled, those providers may use cookies or similar technologies to measure usage, prevent fraud, or show and measure advertisements, subject to their own policies and applicable consent requirements. This policy should be updated to identify the services actually enabled before they are activated.

5. Third-party resources

The website currently loads libraries and fonts from third-party content delivery and font services. When your browser requests those resources, the provider may receive technical request information such as your IP address. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies.

6. Sharing and retention

PDFemWord does not sell the PDF files selected in the current converter. We do not receive those files through that conversion process. Technical logs, if generated by the hosting or delivery provider, are subject to that provider's retention practices.

7. Your choices and security

You can stop using the website at any time and clear locally stored browser data through your browser settings. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep backup copies of important documents and use a trusted device and network.

8. Children

PDFemWord is a general-purpose document utility and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes

We may revise this policy when the website or its services change. The date below indicates the latest revision.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, visit our Contact Us page.
