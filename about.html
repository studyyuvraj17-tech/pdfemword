About Us

Simple, accessible tools to help you work with PDF documents.

What is PDFemWord?

PDFemWord is a web-based toolkit designed to make everyday document tasks simpler. Our goal is to provide straightforward tools that work in your browser, without unnecessary steps or account creation.

What we offer

Our current tool converts text-based PDF documents into editable Microsoft Word (DOCX) files. We aim to make document handling easier for students, professionals, small businesses and anyone who works with PDFs.

How it works

The current converter uses browser-based technology to read text from a selected PDF and create a DOCX file. Conversion is performed on your device in the browser; the selected PDF is not uploaded to a PDFemWord server by this converter.

Our approach

  • Simple: clear tools and an easy-to-follow process.
  • Privacy-conscious: the current converter processes the selected document locally in your browser.
  • Transparent: we explain known limitations, including formatting differences and scanned PDFs that require OCR.
